## 1. Architectural Evolution

The Road Surveillance Incident Detection System transitions from candidate generation into downstream multimodal verification. Crucially, **Qwen3.8-27B does not replace the deterministic detectors**. The deterministic CV engine remains the high-throughput, low-latency candidate generator; Qwen operates downstream on candidate evidence packages to verify physical impacts and suppress false alarms.

## 2. Structured Verification Output Schema

Every candidate episode evaluated by the verifier returns a strict, machine-readable verdict according to `road_surveillance.verification.schema`:

```json
{
  "event_id": "unS0-TLF1ao_00_single_vehicle_40",
  "video_id": "unS0-TLF1ao_00",
  "candidate_frame": 40,
  "verdict": "CONFIRMED_INCIDENT",
  "confidence": 0.94,
  "detected_crash_type": "t_bone",
  "involved_tracks": [1, 2],
  "rationale": "High-speed perpendicular collision observed at intersection. Keyframe sequence shows clear vehicle impact crumple at frame 40 followed by trajectory deflection in post-frame.",
  "telemetry_summary": {
    "detector": "single_vehicle",
    "metric_speed_kmh": 48.2,
    "metric_accel_ms2": -14.6
  }
}
```

### Strict Verdict Taxonomy
1. **`CONFIRMED_INCIDENT`**: Visible physical collision (body crumple, bumper impact, glass/debris dispersion), vehicle rollover/upset, or violent impact into roadway barriers/curbs.
2. **`REJECTED_FALSE_ALARM`**: No physical impact occurs. Examples:
   - *Normal Following*: Vehicles braking in-lane without contact.
   - *Parallel Lane Merge*: Vehicles changing lanes alongside each other with separation.
   - *Crossing Traffic Clearance (Near-Miss)*: Crossing vehicles passing through the same intersection point at slightly different times without touching.
   - *Stationary Wreckage Slideout*: Secondary motion of an already stationary damaged vehicle that was involved in an earlier primary incident episode.
3. **`UNCERTAIN`**: Camera resolution, extreme distance, deep shadow, headlight glare, or severe occlusion prevents definitive visual confirmation.

## 3. Controlled Experiment 1 Protocol

To provide an empirical, unambiguous answer to whether multimodal verification adds value, Experiment 1 is strictly controlled:

1. **Exact Test Split**: The identical 30 frozen test videos (15 in-distribution + 15 geo-aware unseen locations).
2. **Exact Candidate Inputs**: The identical 34 candidate episodes generated by the frozen Phase 1 pipeline:
   - **12 True Positive Candidate Episodes**
   - **22 False Positive Candidate Episodes**
3. **Evidence Packaging**: For every candidate episode, `CandidateEvidenceExtractor` extracts:
   - Frame $t - 15$ (Approach / Pre-conflict)
   - Frame $t$ (Trigger / Conflict point, with red bounding box overlays on involved tracks)
   - Frame $t + 15$ (Resolution / Post-conflict trajectory)
   - Synchronized kinematic telemetry (speeds, decelerations, TCA, DCA, yaw rate, AR change)
4. **Primary Research Questions**:
   - **Recall Retention**: How many of the 12 TP incidents remain `CONFIRMED_INCIDENT`? (Target: 100% retention; measure any false rejection).
   - **FP Suppression**: How many of the 22 FP episodes are `REJECTED_FALSE_ALARM`? (Measure actual suppression rate rather than assuming near-zero).
   - **Uncertainty Rate**: How many episodes fall into `UNCERTAIN`?
   - **Net F1 and Precision**: What is the net shift in Precision, Recall, F1, and FP episodes per video?

### Operational Decision Policies for `UNCERTAIN`
- **Policy A (Conservative ITS Operator)**: Treat `UNCERTAIN` as positive alerts for human review.
  $$\text{TP}_A = \text{TP}_{\text{confirmed}} + \text{TP}_{\text{uncertain}}, \quad \text{FP}_A = \text{FP}_{\text{confirmed}} + \text{FP}_{\text{uncertain}}$$
- **Policy B (Selective Automated Dispatch)**: Only escalate alerts with `CONFIRMED_INCIDENT`.
  $$\text{TP}_B = \text{TP}_{\text{confirmed}}, \quad \text{FP}_B = \text{FP}_{\text{confirmed}}$$

## 4. Implementation Manifest

1. **`road_surveillance/verification/schema.py`**:
   - `VerificationVerdict` enum (`CONFIRMED_INCIDENT`, `REJECTED_FALSE_ALARM`, `UNCERTAIN`).
   - `IncidentVerificationResult` structured dataclass with serialization.
2. **`road_surveillance/verification/base.py`**:
   - `CandidateEvidencePackage` data structure.
   - `IncidentVerifier` abstract interface.
3. **`road_surveillance/verification/evidence_extractor.py`**:
   - `CandidateEvidenceExtractor` keyframe seek, extraction, bounding box overlay, and base64 JPEG encoding.
4. **`road_surveillance/verification/qwen_verifier.py`**:
   - `QwenMultimodalVerifier` supporting OpenAI-compatible endpoints (vLLM, Ollama, OpenRouter, ModelScope, DashScope), JSON parsing, and fault-tolerant fallbacks.
5. **`road_surveillance/config/`**:
   - `VerificationConfig` integrated into `config_loader.py` and `default_config.yaml`.
6. **`tests/test_verification.py`**:
   - 6 unit tests covering schema serialization, evidence packaging, base64 encoding, mock mode, JSON variant parsing, and connection failure handling (**37/37 total tests passing**).
7. **`scripts/benchmark_phase2_qwen_verification.py`**:
   - Full evaluation harness processing all 30 videos and 34 candidate episodes.
